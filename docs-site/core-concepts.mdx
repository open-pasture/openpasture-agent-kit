# Core Concepts

Openpasture asks one question every decision window, for every herd: should
the herd move today, and if so, where? Everything below exists to answer that
well and to remember what happened.

The farmer remains the operator. The engine carries the daily attention and
the long memory.

## Farm

The top-level management unit. A farm has a timezone, a location, and the land
units, herds, animals, and infrastructure on it.

## Land Unit

The shared map shape behind pastures, paddocks, and sections. A **pasture** is
a durable field. A **paddock** is a grazing subdivision inside it. A
**section** is an optional temporary strip inside a paddock. Paddock geometry
is what the engine uses to request land reports and to build boundaries.

## Herd

A group of animals managed under one decision. A herd tracks species, count,
current paddock, the collars it wears, and its autonomy setting.

## Observation

A time-bound signal about the farm. A farmer note, a photo, a weather reading,
and one section of a land report are all observations, each with its source.

## Land Report

What Alexandria returns about a point or polygon: weather, imagery, climate,
hazards, soil, vegetation, species, water, and terrain. Each section carries
its sources or says plainly that it is unavailable. See
[Land Reports](/land-reports).

## Collar Report And Boundary Command

Collars send position fixes, cue events, and battery. The engine sends a
**boundary command**: a versioned polygon, optional exclusions, and an optional
transition corridor. A boundary is active only once the collars confirm it.
See [Collars](/collars).

## Grazing Signals

Calculations over stored data: grazing pressure, rest days, forage, recovery,
feed budget, weather risk, and herd behavior. Signals are evidence for the
decision, not the decision itself.

## Grazing Decision

The decision for one herd in one window.

- `STAY`: stay inside the current boundary.
- `MOVE`: move to a specific land unit and boundary.
- `NEEDS_INFO`: the call depends on one observation the data cannot give.

A decision carries its reasons, a confidence, the inputs it used, the autonomy
in force, the farmer's response, the boundary sent, and the outcome evaluated
on later cycles. See [Decision Engine](/decision-engine).

## Autonomy

The farmer's setting for how far the engine may act on its own.

- `propose`: every move waits for approval. The default.
- `apply_unless_stopped`: a move is sent at a set time unless the farmer stops
  it.
- `apply`: moves are sent when decided. The farmer reviews after.

## The Decision Record

Every decision, the inputs behind it, the farmer's response, the boundary
sent, and the outcome that followed. This is the core asset. Over time it
shows how this farm's land responds to how it is grazed. When the farmer
overrides a decision, the override is kept with the original. It is signal,
not error.

## Morning Brief

The farmer-readable wrapper of the latest decision for each herd. It says what
the herd should do, why, what was sent to the collars, and what would help
next.

## Knowledge Entry

A structured lesson from a trusted source, with attribution. Types are
`principle`, `technique`, `signal`, and `mistake`. Decisions cite the entries
that shaped them. See [Knowledge](/knowledge).
