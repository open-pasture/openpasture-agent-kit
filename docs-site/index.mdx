# Openpasture

Openpasture is the data and decision layer between what the world knows about
your land and the collars that move your herd.

Every day, for every herd, it answers one question:

> Should the herd move today, and if so, where?

It works that out from the land, the animals, and your farm's own history. It
tells you why. When you allow it, it sends the new boundary to the collars and
checks that they got it. You stay the operator.

## Two things it sits between

- **Alexandria** knows the land. Give it a point or a paddock and it returns
  current, past, and forecast weather, the latest satellite imagery, drought
  and climate history, hazards, soil, vegetation, species, water, and terrain,
  each with its sources. It also carries the grazing knowledge: research,
  practitioner videos, and the hands-on wisdom of graziers like Greg Judy.
- **OpenCollar** knows where the animals are. Collars report each animal's
  position and accept boundary updates that move the herd.

Openpasture holds what neither one has: your farm's structure, the
calculations that turn raw data into grazing signals, and the record of every
decision.

## The daily loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assemble">
    Collar positions since the last decision, a land report for the current
    paddock and the candidates, your grazing history, and relevant knowledge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Derive">
    Grazing pressure, rest days, forage, recovery, a feed budget against the
    forecast, and weather risk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    `STAY`, `MOVE` to a specific boundary, or `NEEDS_INFO`. With reasons, a
    confidence, and the one observation that would help most.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    You approve, change, or override. How much review is required is your
    setting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act">
    The boundary goes to the collars. Openpasture confirms each collar received
    it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn">
    Whether the herd held the line, how they grazed, how the paddock recovered.
    That becomes tomorrow's context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You decide how much to hand over

- `propose`: every move waits for your approval. This is the default.
- `apply_unless_stopped`: a move is sent at a set time unless you stop it.
- `apply`: moves are sent when decided. You review after.

An override is never an error. It is the most useful signal the system gets,
and it is kept with the decision it replaced.

## Where to start

- [Quickstart](/quickstart): install the kit and run a first decision.
- [Core Concepts](/core-concepts): herds, decisions, autonomy, and the record.
- [Decision Engine](/decision-engine): how one decision is made and recorded.

The engine works with any capable agent through the [CLI](/cli),
[MCP](/mcp-connector), or [Hermes](/hermes-adapter). The farm view, with the
map and decision timeline, is part of [Hosted Cloud](/hosted-cloud).

<Info>
  Land reports and collars ship with offline stand-ins: fixture land reports and
  a simulated collar gateway. The whole loop runs without hardware or accounts.
  Real land data needs Alexandria configured. Real collars need the HTTP
  gateway. See [Land Reports](/land-reports) and [Collars](/collars).
</Info>
