# Hosted Cloud

Don't want to self-host? The hosted Openpasture product runs the open-source
engine for you and adds the farm view: the map, the decision timeline, and
notifications when a decision is waiting on you.

<Info>
  The hosted product is in beta. [Get started at openpasture.dev →](https://app.openpasture.dev/sign-up)
</Info>

## How it works

When you sign up, the hosted product runs the engine for your farm with its own
isolated data. The farm view shows the herd on the map with today's boundary
and the proposed next one, the timeline of decisions with their reasons, and
each paddock's history and recovery. You approve, change, or override a
decision right on the map.

The farm view reads the decision record through the same kit tools documented
on this site, and your answers go back through them. Decisions, autonomy, and
what gets sent to the collars all come from the open-source engine. The hosted
product adds the infrastructure and the view, not the reasoning.
